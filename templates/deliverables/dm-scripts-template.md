# DM Scripts Template

## SCRIPT 1 — Post Engager DM

**Trigger:** Person comments on your post
**Goal:** Continue conversation, build relationship
**Length:** 2-3 lines

[Specific thing they said about your post] — totally agree (or [your take]).
[One follow-up question or observation]?

**Example:**
"Your point about cold email deliverability is spot on — I've been testing a new approach that got 3x the reply rate.
Have you tried segmenting by industry before?"

---

## SCRIPT 2 — Lead Magnet Delivery DM

**Trigger:** Person commented the trigger word for lead magnet
**Goal:** Deliver lead magnet + open conversation
**Length:** 3-4 lines

Hey [name]! Here's the [lead magnet name]: [link]
[One sentence on what they'll get]
[One question to open conversation]?

**Example:**
Hey Sarah! Here's the Cold Email Blueprint: [link]
Inside you'll find the exact 3-step sequence I used to book 12 calls/month.
What's your biggest challenge with outbound right now?

---

## SCRIPT 3 — Profile Viewer DM

**Trigger:** Someone viewed your LinkedIn profile
**Goal:** Start dialogue, zero pitch
**Length:** 2 lines

Hey [name] — [specific thing about their work that caught your attention].
[One question that shows genuine interest]?

**Example:**
Hey Mike — love that you're running content-led growth at a Series A. Curious, are you doing mostly organic or mixing in paid?

---

## SCRIPT 4 — Connection Request Follow-Up

**Trigger:** 24 hours after connection accepted
**Goal:** Get a reply, not a sale
**Length:** 3 lines

Hey [name]! Thanks for connecting.
[One specific thing about their profile/work that resonated].
[One question or conversation starter]?

**Example:**
Hey Lisa! Thanks for connecting. Loved your post about B2B content calendars — the template approach is genius.
Are you currently using any automation for content distribution?

---

## SCRIPT 5 — Warm Lead Nurture DM

**Trigger:** Person has engaged multiple times (comments, views, replies)
**Goal:** Transition to call or paid offer
**Length:** 4-5 lines

Hey [name] — I've noticed you [specific engagement pattern].
[Why you're reaching out specifically].
[Soft offer introduction]: I actually [context about your offer].
[Call to action or question]?

**Example:**
Hey Tom — I've seen you engaging with a few of my posts on outbound strategy lately. Seems like you might be thinking about scaling your pipeline.
I actually just opened up a few spots for 1:1 coaching on this exact topic — would you be open to a quick chat about what's working?

---

## SCRIPT 6 — Re-engagement DM

**Trigger:** Warm lead went cold (30+ days no response)
**Goal:** Re-open conversation
**Length:** 2 lines

Hey [name] — [reference to past engagement or shared context].
[New value or perspective]?

**Example:**
Hey Sarah — I know we connected a while back about cold email strategy. I just published something on a completely different approach that's been getting solid results — happy to share if useful?

---

## Trigger Conditions Summary

| Script | Trigger | When to Use |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| 1 | Post comment | Every comment |
| 2 | Comment trigger word | Immediately after |
| 3 | Profile view | Within 24-48 hours |
| 4 | Connection accepted | 24 hours after |
| 5 | Multiple engagements | After 3+ engagements |
| 6 | 30+ days no response | After 30 days cold |
