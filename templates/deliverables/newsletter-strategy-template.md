## Section 3: Monetization Ladder

### Weeks 1-4: Build Trust
Focus: Pure value, deliver wins
CTAs: Lead magnet only, no paid offers
Goal: Build open rates and trust

### Weeks 5-8: Introduce Paid Offer
Focus: Story/case study introduction of offer
CTAs: Soft mention via story, lead magnet still primary
Goal: Plant seeds, gauge interest

### Weeks 9-12: Direct Pitch Cycle
Focus: 3-email sequence embedded in newsletter
CTAs: Direct offer, urgency, limited spots
Goal: Convert readers to customers
