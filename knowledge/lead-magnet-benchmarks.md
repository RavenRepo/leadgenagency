## What Increases CVR

1. **Specificity**: Named numbers, timeframes, audiences
2. **Immediate value**: Quick win in first section
3. **Visual design**: Professional, not generic
4. **Social proof**: Testimonials, logos, data
5. **Urgency**: Limited spots, closing soon
6. **Friction reduction**: Fewer form fields, easy access

## What Decreases CVR

1. **Generic titles**: "Ultimate Guide to..." (overused)
2. **No quick win**: Too much setup before value
3. **Design debt**: Looks like a PDF from 2009
4. **No proof**: No testimonials or data
5. **Long form**: 40+ pages for a checklist
6. **Misalignment**: Promise doesn't match content
