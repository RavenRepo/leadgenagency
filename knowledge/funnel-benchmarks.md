|--------|-----------------|

| Metric | Poor | Average | Good | Great |
|--------|------|---------|------|-------|

## LTV Benchmarks

### By Business Type
| Type | Good LTV | Great LTV | Notes |
|------|----------|-----------|-------|
| Coaching (high ticket) | 3x initial | 5x+ | Upsell to group, mastermind |
| SaaS (monthly) | 12 months | 24+ months | Focus on retention |
| SaaS (annual) | 2x initial | 3x+ | Upgrade path critical |
| Info Product | 1x (one-time) | 2x+ | Upsell, continuity |
| Agency | 3-5x initial | 8x+ | Retainers, scope expansion |
| E-commerce | 2-3x COGS | 4-5x+ | Focus on LTV, repeat purchases |

### Key Retention Levers
1. **Onboarding**: First 7 days set the tone
2. **Community**: Reduces churn, increases LTV
3. **Upsells**: Higher LTV per customer
4. **Renewals/Retainers**: Predictable revenue

## Funnel Drop-off Points

### Common Leak Points
1. **Post-opt-in**: Confusing next steps
2. **Day 2-3**: Email becomes irrelevant
3. **Week 2**: No clear path to offer
4. **Offer page**: Price mismatch with perceived value

### Fix Strategies
| Drop-off Point | Fix |
|---------------|-----|
| Post-opt-in | Clear CTA on thank you page |
| Day 2-3 | Send high-value email immediately |
| Week 2 | Clear nurture path, engagement emails |
| Offer page | Better match between lead magnet and offer |
