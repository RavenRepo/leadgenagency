# DM Response Rate Benchmarks

Performance data for DM scripts by script type, ICP, and industry.

| Script Type | Avg Response Rate | Top-Performing Rate | Notes |
|-------------|-------------------|---------------------|-------|
| Post Engager | 35-50% | 60%+ | Highest engagement |
| Lead Magnet Delivery | 25-40% | 55%+ | High intent |
| Profile Viewer | 15-25% | 35%+ | Cold but curious |
| Connection Follow-Up | 30-45% | 55%+ | Relationship start |
| Warm Lead Nurture | 20-30% | 40%+ | Existing interest |
| Re-engagement | 8-15% | 25%+ | Cold list revival |

## By Script Type

### Post Engager DM
- **Response rate**: 35-50%
- **Conversion to call**: 5-10%
- **Best for**: Building relationships, warming ICP
- **Key to success**: Reference something specific from their comment

### Lead Magnet Delivery
- **Response rate**: 25-40%
- **Conversion to call**: 10-20%
- **Best for**: Starting conversations with warm leads
- **Key to success**: Ask a question about their situation, not about your offer

### Profile Viewer
- **Response rate**: 15-25%
- **Conversion to call**: 3-8%
- **Best for**: Expanding reach into new ICP segments
- **Key to success**: Reference something specific about their work

### Connection Follow-Up
- **Response rate**: 30-45%
- **Conversion to call**: 8-15%
- **Best for**: Converting new connections to relationships
- **Key to success**: Don't pitch — give value first

### Warm Lead Nurture
- **Response rate**: 20-30%
- **Conversion to call**: 15-25%
- **Best for**: Moving engaged prospects to sales conversation
- **Key to success**: Timing — wait for multiple engagements

### Re-engagement
- **Response rate**: 8-15%
- **Conversion to call**: 2-5%
- **Best for**: Reactivating old leads
- **Key to success**: New value or perspective

## Factors That Increase Response Rate

1. **Specificity**: Reference something unique about them
2. **Question quality**: One clear question, not interrogation
3. **Timing**: Send when they're likely online
4. **Value-first**: Give something before asking
5. **Personalization**: Mention their content, company, situation

## Factors That Decrease Response Rate

1. **Generic openers**: "Hi, I help companies like yours..."
2. **Multiple questions**: Bombarding with questions
3. **Immediate pitch**: Trying to sell in first DM
4. **Wrong ICP**: Messaging people who don't match
5. **Copy-paste feel**: Not enough personalization
