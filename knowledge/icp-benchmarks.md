# ICP Benchmarks by Industry

Benchmark patterns for Ideal Customer Profiles across common industries.

## SaaS (B2B)

### Typical ICP Demographics
- **Job titles**: VP Marketing, Head of Growth, Marketing Manager, Founder
- **Company size**: 10-200 employees, Series A-C
- **Geography**: US, UK, English-speaking markets

### Typical Psychographics
- **Desire**: Scalable growth, predictable revenue, efficient customer acquisition
- **Fear**: Market saturation, losing to competitors, runway pressure
- **Frustration**: CAC too high, funnel leaks, no reliable playbook

### Proven VOC Phrases
- "I don't know where my next 100 customers are coming from"
- "We're growing but burning cash faster"
- "Content marketing feels like throwing spaghetti at the wall"

### Watering Holes
- Indie Hackers, Hacker News, SaaStr, Product Hunt
- LinkedIn groups: SaaS Growth, B2B Marketing, Startup Growth

## Coaching / Consulting

### Typical ICP Demographics
- **Job titles**: CEO, Founder, Managing Director, Executive Coach
- **Company size**: 1-50 employees, revenue $500K-$5M
- **Geography**: US, UK, AU, Canada

### Typical Psychographics
- **Desire**: Scale without trading time for money, premium positioning
- **Fear**: Hitting a ceiling, being seen as just "another coach"
- **Frustration**: Inconsistent pipeline, feast-or-famine months

### Proven VOC Phrases
- "I want to grow but I can't do everything myself"
- "I'm great at my craft but terrible at sales"
- "I keep giving away free advice"

### Watering Holes
- LinkedIn posts about entrepreneurship, EO groups, YPO
- Podcasts: "How I Built This," "The Tim Ferriss Show"

## Agency (Marketing / Creative)

### Typical ICP Demographics
- **Job titles**: Founder, Managing Partner, Director of Strategy
- **Company size**: 5-100 employees, revenue $1M-$20M
- **Geography**: US, UK, English-speaking markets

### Typical Psychographics
- **Desire**: Land bigger clients, escape client dependency, build retainer revenue
- **Fear**: Losing key clients, being replaced by AI tools, margin compression
- **Frustration**: Scope creep, late payments, client education burden

### Proven VOC Phrases
- "We're always chasing new business"
- "My team is maxed out but revenue isn't scaling"
- "I can't seem to raise my prices"

### Watering Holes
- LinkedIn: Agency Growth, Creative Directors group
- Podcasts: "Agency Growth Podcast," "Building a Storybrand"

## E-commerce / DTC Brand

### Typical ICP Demographics
- **Job titles**: Founder, CEO, Head of Marketing, E-commerce Manager
- **Company size**: $500K-$10M revenue, 5-100 employees
- **Geography**: US, UK, CA, AU

### Typical Psychographics
- **Desire**: Scalable customer acquisition, brand differentiation, repeat purchasers
- **Fear**: Reliance on paid ads, Amazon dependency, competition
- **Frustration**: Low LTV, high return rates, algorithm changes

### Proven VOC Phrases
- "We're profitable but dependent on Meta ads"
- "I don't know how to build a real brand"
- "Customer acquisition costs keep going up"

### Watering Holes
- LinkedIn E-commerce groups, DTC brands communities
- Podcasts: "My Wife Quit Her Job," "E-commerce Fuel"
