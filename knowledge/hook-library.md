# Hook Library

High-performing hook formulas organized by post type. These are proven structures — use them as starting points, then add your specific twist.

## Authority Post Hooks

### Framework Hook
"The [X] framework that [result] in [timeframe]:"

Examples:
- "The 3-email sequence that books 8 calls/month in 60 days:"
- "The outreach framework that got me 23 replies in one week:"

### Anti-Pattern Hook
"Stop doing [common behavior]. Do this instead."

Examples:
- "Stop sending cold emails that sound cold. Do this instead."
- "Stop posting content that nobody reads. Do this instead."

### Checklist Hook
"[Number] things most [ICP] get wrong about [topic]:"

Examples:
- "5 things most founders get wrong about B2B content:"
- "3 misconceptions about cold outreach that cost you replies:"

### Process Hook
"The exact [process] I used to [specific result]:"

Examples:
- "The exact 5-step LinkedIn outreach process I used to book 15 calls:"
- "The exact content repurposing system I built for 3x more reach:"

## Story Post Hooks

### Mistake Hook
"[Years] ago I made a mistake that cost me [$/outcome]:"

Examples:
- "4 years ago I made a mistake that cost my agency $180K:"
- "I almost gave up on content marketing because of this mistake:"

### Realization Hook
"The moment I realized [truth about their situation]:"

Examples:
- "The moment I realized why my cold emails weren't converting:"
- "The moment I understood why my content wasn't driving leads:"

### Client Hook
"A client told me something last week that changed how I think about [topic]:"

Examples:
- "A client told me something that changed how I think about pricing:"
- "A startup founder told me why they didn't hire me — and it hurt:"

## Proof Post Hooks

### Result Hook
"[Result] in [timeframe] — here's what we did:"

Examples:
- "378 qualified leads in 30 days — here's exactly what we did:"
- "From 0 to $47K MRR in 9 months — the playbook:"

### Before/After Hook
"[Metric] went from [before] to [after] — the [framework/approach]:"

Examples:
- "Reply rate went from 2% to 11% — the email rewrite that did it:"
- "LinkedIn engagement went from 1% to 8% — here's why:"

### Client Hook
"[Client name] just [specific result]:"

Examples:
- "This client just hit $100K MRR — here's what changed:"
- "One founder went from 5 demos/month to 18 — here's what shifted:"

## Engagement Post Hooks

### Unpopular Opinion
"Unpopular opinion: [contrarian take]"

Examples:
- "Unpopular opinion: content marketing doesn't work for most B2B companies"
- "Hot take: cold email is not dead — it's just done wrong"

### Poll Question
"[Poll question with clear options]"

Examples:
- "Which grows faster: content or outbound? (I've done both)"
- "What's your biggest challenge with lead generation right now?"

### "Who Else" Pattern
"Who else is experiencing [common frustration]?"

Examples:
- "Who else is tired of 'thought leadership' that says nothing?"
- "Who else is struggling to get sales to actually follow up on leads?"

## Direct Offer Hooks

### Outcome Hook
"[Specific result] is closer than you think — here's how:"

Examples:
- "More qualified leads is closer than you think — here's how:"
- "Your first 100 customers are closer than you think — here's the path:"

### Resource Hook
"[Resource name]: [what it is] for [specific audience]"

Examples:
- "The Cold Email Blueprint: 47 pages of scripts, templates, and sequences"
- "The Lead Gen Audit Template: find your biggest funnel leak in 20 minutes"

---

## What Makes a Hook Work

1. **Specificity**: Numbers, names, timeframes — not vague
2. **Curiosity gap**: Creates need to know more
3. **Self-reference**: "Most [my audience]" — immediately relevant
4. **Emotion**: Taps into frustration, desire, or curiosity
5. **Credibility signal**: Shows you have the answer

## Hooks to Avoid

1. "I want to share..." — too soft
2. "Here's a tip..." — too generic
3. "You might be wondering..." — forced curiosity
4. Questions that don't create genuine curiosity
5. "Ultimate guide" type titles — overused
