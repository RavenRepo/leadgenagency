# Funnel Architecture Skill

Funnel stage definitions, tech stack tiers, KPI benchmark tables for the funnel-architect agent.

## The 5 Stages

### Stage 1: Awareness (TOFU)
Goal: Drive traffic and attention
Channels: Content, social posts, cold outreach, ads, referrals
CTA strategy:
- Comment-trigger: "Comment [word] for [lead magnet]"
- Link posts: Soft content CTA to landing page
- Story: Swipe up / Link in bio

### Stage 2: Opt-in (MOFU)
Goal: Convert visitor to email subscriber
Landing page: Dedicated page with lead magnet offer
Thank you page: Deliver lead magnet + set next expectation
Success metric: 30%+ opt-in rate (above benchmark)

### Stage 3: Delivery + Indoctrination (D1-D3)
Goal: Deliver lead magnet, begin relationship, build trust
Email cadence:
- D0: Deliver lead magnet + 1-sentence expectation setter
- D1: Quick win — deliver one key insight from lead magnet
- D2: Story — your journey, client's journey, or relevant case
- D3: Soft proof — introduce paid offer via case study

### Stage 4: Nurture (D4-D14)
Goal: Build relationship, demonstrate expertise, plant seeds for offer
Email cadence: Every 2-3 days
Content ratio: 3 value : 2 story : 2 proof : 1 offer
Value types: Tips, frameworks, insights, tools
Story types: Client wins, lessons learned, behind-the-scenes
Proof types: Testimonials, case studies, results screenshots

### Stage 5: Conversion
Goal: Drive to paid offer
Primary CTA: Clear offer with urgency
Objection handling: Address in nurture emails before presenting offer
Re-engagement: For non-openers, send different subject line sequence

## KPI Benchmarks

### Email Metrics
| Metric | Poor | Average | Good | Great |
|--------|------|---------|------|-------|
| Open rate | <15% | 20-25% | 30%+ | 40%+ |
| Click rate | <2% | 2-4% | 5%+ | 8%+ |
| Unsubscribe rate | >0.5% | 0.2-0.5% | <0.2% | <0.1% |

### Landing Page Metrics
| Metric | Poor | Average | Good | Great |
|--------|------|---------|------|-------|
| Opt-in rate | <2% | 3-5% | 8%+ | 15%+ |
| Bounce rate | >70% | 50-70% | 30-50% | <30% |

### Funnel Conversion
| Stage | Benchmark |
|-------|-----------|
| Awareness → Traffic | Depends on channel |
| Traffic → Opt-in | 3-10% |
| Opt-in → Email Open (D1) | 50-70% |
| Email Open → Click (D1) | 10-20% |
| Nurture → Offer View | 15-25% |
| Offer View → Purchase | 2-5% |

### LTV Benchmarks
| Business Type | Good LTV | Great LTV |
|----------------|----------|-----------|
| Coaching | 3x initial | 5x+ |
| SaaS (monthly) | 12 months | 24+ months |
| Info product | 1x (one-time) | 2x (upsell) |
| Agency | 3-5x initial | 8x+ |

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] All 5 stages defined with explicit CTAs
- [ ] Traffic source strategy per channel
- [ ] Thank you page strategy specified
- [ ] Email cadence and content mix defined
- [ ] Tech stack recommendation within budget tier

- [ ] Re-engagement sequence planned
