# Post Type Selection Skill

5 post types defined, hook formulas, platform-specific formatting for the content-strategist and post-writer agents.

## The 5 Post Types

### TYPE 1: Authority Posts (25%)

Purpose: Position you as the expert in your niche
Content: Educational frameworks, systems, processes
Tone: Teaching, generous, no fluff
CTA: "Comment [X] for my [lead magnet]" or "Link in first comment"

Example structure:
- Hook: Name the framework/system
- Body: 3-5 steps or key components
- CTA: Request a comment or save

### TYPE 2: Story Posts (20%)

Purpose: Build human connection and emotional resonance
Content: Personal or client story with a lesson
Tone: Vulnerable, specific, relatable
CTA: "Save this / Share with someone who needs this"

Example structure:
- Hook: The moment or situation (specific)
- Body: What happened, what you learned
- CTA: Emotional resonance (save/share)

### TYPE 3: Proof Posts (20%)

Purpose: Demonstrate social proof and credibility
Content: Screenshots, results, testimonials, before/afters
Tone: Confident, specific, let results speak
CTA: "DM me if you want this for your business"

Example structure:
- Hook: The result in specific numbers
- Body: Brief context, methodology
- CTA: Soft ask for DMs

### TYPE 4: Engagement Posts (20%)

Purpose: Drive comments and algorithm boost
Content: Polls, contrarian takes, hot takes, questions
Tone: Provocative, curious, conversational
CTA: "Comment below / What do you think?"

Example structure:
- Hook: The contrarian view or provocative question
- Body: Why you believe this (brief)
- CTA: Question that drives comments

### TYPE 5: Direct Offer Posts (15%)

Purpose: Soft pitch to lead magnet or paid offer
Content: Lead magnet topic, soft offer introduction
Tone: Clear, direct, no apology
CTA: "Comment [X] / Link in bio"

Example structure:
- Hook: The outcome the lead magnet delivers
- Body: What's inside, who it's for
- CTA: Direct to lead magnet

## Hook Formulas

### Authority Hooks
- "The [X] framework that [result] in [timeframe]:"
- "Stop doing [common behavior]. Do this instead."
- "3 things most [ICP] get wrong about [topic]:"
- "The exact [process] I used to [result]:"

### Story Hooks
- "X years ago I made a mistake that cost me $Y:"
- "I almost gave up until [pivot point]:"
- "A client told me something last week that changed how I think about [topic]:"
- "The moment I realized [truth about their situation]:"

### Proof Hooks
- "[Result] in [timeframe] — here's what we did:"
- "This [screenshot/chart] shows [specific outcome]:"
- "[Number] clients later, the pattern is clear:"
- "From $X to $Y ARR in [timeframe]:"

### Engagement Hooks
- "Unpopular opinion: [contrarian take]"
- "Hot take: [provocative statement]"
- "[Poll question]: [Options]"
- "Who else is experiencing this? [situation]"

## Platform Formatting

### LinkedIn
- Max: 3000 characters
- Break at 200 chars for "see more" fold
- Hashtag block at END (not in body)
- Ideal length: 1200-1800 chars
- Best posting times: 7-8am, 12pm, 5-6pm ET
- Line breaks for scanability

### X/Twitter
- Max: 280 characters per tweet
- Thread format for longer content
- Hook tweet: Stop the scroll
- Thread: 3-5 tweets max
- Best times: 8am, 12pm, 3pm ET

### Instagram
- Caption: 150-300 words optimal
- Hook line critical (shows in preview)
- CTA: "Link in bio"
- Hashtags: 8-15 in comments

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Post type ratios correct (25/20/20/20/15)
- [ ] Every post has a clear CTA
- [ ] Hook passes scroll-stop test
- [ ] Body delivers promised value
- [ ] CTA is singular (one action only)
- [ ] LinkedIn posts break at 200 chars
- [ ] X posts under 280 chars (or threaded)
- [ ] Lead magnet CTA appears 2x+ per week
