# Lead Magnet Match Skill

Format scoring matrix, title formulas, and connection-to-offer frameworks for the lead-magnet-strategist agent.

## Format Scoring Matrix

Score each format 1-5 on:
- Audience sophistication: does ICP want deep content or quick wins?
- Offer complexity: is the paid offer simple or complex?
- Platform context: where will they find this lead magnet?

### The 10 Formats

| Format | Audience Soph. | Offer Complexity | Platform | Best For |
|--------|---------------|-----------------|----------|----------|
| Checklist | 2 | 1 | LinkedIn, Email | Busy professionals, simple problems |
| Template | 3 | 2 | LinkedIn, Email | Practical ICP, process-heavy offers |
| Swipe File | 3 | 2 | LinkedIn | Creatives, marketers |
| Mini-course | 4 | 3 | Email, FB | Complex topics, higher ticket |
| Video Training | 3 | 3 | LinkedIn, YouTube | Visual learners, tutorial-heavy |
| Calculator/Tool | 4 | 4 | Landing page | Data-driven ICP, immediate value |
| Report/Blueprint | 4 | 2 | LinkedIn, Email | Thought leaders, serious buyers |
| Email Challenge | 3 | 2 | Email | Engagement-focused, community |
| Webinar/Workshop | 3 | 3 | LinkedIn, Email | High-ticket, complex sales |
| Community Access | 3 | 3 | LinkedIn | Network-focused, peer learning |

### Score Calculation
- Total each format across 3 dimensions
- Top 2 scores = finalist formats
- Pick winner based on ICP fit, not just score

## Title Formulas

### Formula 1: Specific Result
"[Specific result] for [specific audience] in [timeframe] without [key objection]"

Examples:
- "3x More Qualified Leads for B2B Founders in 90 Days Without Cold Calling"
- "The Outreach System That Books 10 Calls/Week for Solo Consultants Without Buying Ads"

### Formula 2: The Counter-Intuitive
"Why [commonly believed thing] is costing you [problem] — and what to do instead"

Examples:
- "Why Your Content Calendar is Killing Your Engagement (And What to Do Instead)"
- "Why You're Losing Deals in the Demo (And It's Not Your Pitch)"

### Formula 3: The Finish Line
"The exact [outcome] that [specific audience] gets in [timeframe] — here's how"

Examples:
- "The Exact 5-Step System That Gets SaaS Founders to $10K/Month — Here's How"
- "The Pipeline Review Process That Closes 40% of Qualified Leads — Inside Look"

### Title Validation
Does the title pass?
- Specific: does it name a real number, timeframe, person?
- Desirable: would the ICP want this outcome?
- Believable: would a skeptic believe it's possible?
- Copyable: can a copywriter use this as-is?

## Connection to Offer Frameworks

### Bridge Type 1: Teaser
Lead magnet shows ONE element of the paid offer, promises more.

### Bridge Type 2: Foundation
Lead magnet teaches a prerequisite concept. Paid offer is the complete system.

### Bridge Type 3: Case Study
Lead magnet shows results without methodology. Paid offer is the how-to.

### Bridge Type 4: Tool
Lead magnet provides a simplified tool. Paid offer is the full platform.

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Format choice justified against all 3 dimensions
- [ ] Title passes all 4 validation tests
- [ ] Outline has 3-7 sections/pages/modules
- [ ] Promise statement is specific and believable
- [ ] Bridge to paid offer is clear and natural
- [ ] Alternate titles are genuinely different approaches
