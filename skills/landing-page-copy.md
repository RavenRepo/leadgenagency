# Landing Page Copy Skill

9-section structure, headline formulas, CTA copy patterns, CVR benchmarks for the landing-page-copywriter agent.

## The 9-Section Structure

### Section 1: Headline (Above Fold)
One line. ICP's #1 desire + specificity signal.
- NO: "Grow your business"
- YES: "Book 15 sales calls/month without cold outreach"

Formula: [Specific outcome] for [specific ICP] without [key objection]

### Section 2: Subheadline
What they get + who it's for + time/effort qualifier.
- Format: "[Specific deliverable] for [ICP] who want [outcome] in [timeframe]"
- Length: 1-2 lines max

### Section 3: Hero Bullet Block
5 bullets using "You'll discover..." or "Inside you'll find..."
- Each bullet: one specific result, not a feature
- Specificity: names numbers, timeframes, outcomes
- NO: "Save time" → YES: "Cut reporting time from 4 hours to 20 minutes"

### Section 4: Social Proof Block
Testimonials + what proof to collect.
- Format: Quote / Name / Role / Company / Result
- Collect: 2-3 testimonials, screen recordings, case studies
- Placeholder format until real proof exists

### Section 5: What's Inside
Scannable breakdown of lead magnet sections with mini-benefits.
- List each section/module
- Under each: one sentence on the specific value
- Use checkmarks or bullets for scanability

### Section 6: Who This Is For
3-4 bullets: inclusion + exclusion criteria.
- "This is for you if..." (2-3 qualifying traits)
- "This is NOT for you if..." (1-2 disqualifying traits)
- Creates specificity and reduces irrelevant leads

### Section 7: About [Name]
3-line credibility paragraph.
- Results-first: what you've achieved for others
- Credibility second: brief background that supports results
- NO: "Hi, I'm X. I've been doing Y for Z years."
- YES: "I've helped 47 B2B founders book more calls without cold email..."

### Section 8: Final CTA
Repeat headline promise + urgency/access framing.
- Repeat the core desire in CTA language
- Add: limited access framing, bonus, or time element
- Options: "Get the Blueprint" / "Send Me the Checklist" / "Inside: [title]"

### Section 9: CTA Button Copy
5 variants to A/B test:
1. "Get Instant Access"
2. "Send Me the Blueprint"
3. "Get My Free [Title]"
4. "Claim My Copy"
5. "Yes, I Want [Specific Result]"

## Headline Formulas

### Formula 1: Outcome + Audience + Time
"[Outcome] for [Audience] in [Timeframe]"
Example: "15 Qualified Calls/Month for B2B Founders in 8 Weeks"

### Formula 2: Problem + Without
"[Problem solved] without [objection]"
Example: "Consistent Sales Pipeline Without Cold Calling"

### Formula 3: The Results Ladder
"From [current state] to [desired state] in [timeframe]"
Example: "From 0 to 20 Demo Calls/Month in 60 Days"

## CVR Benchmarks

| Industry | Good CVR | Great CVR | Notes |
|----------|----------|-----------|-------|
| SaaS | 3-5% | 7%+ | Typically 5-10% on email capture |
| Coaching | 5-10% | 15%+ | Higher engagement, specific ICP |
| Info Products | 10-15% | 20%+ | Lower barrier, broad appeal |
| B2B Services | 2-4% | 6%+ | Longer cycle, specific targeting |

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Headline passes "so what" test
- [ ] Subheadline names specific deliverable + ICP
- [ ] All 5 hero bullets name results, not features
- [ ] Social proof block present (even as placeholder)
- [ ] "Who's this for" has inclusion AND exclusion
- [ ] About section is results-first
- [ ] Final CTA repeats core promise
- [ ] 5 CTA button variants provided
- [ ] Meta description 160 chars max
