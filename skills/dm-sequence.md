# DM Sequence Skill

DM principles, 6 script types, trigger conditions, conversation flow for the dm-copywriter agent.

## Core DM Principles

### Principle 1: Never Pitch in the First DM
- First touch is for starting a conversation
- Goal: Get a reply, not a sale
- Exception: If they explicitly asked about your offer

### Principle 2: Feel Handwritten, Not Templated
- Vary sentence length
- Use contractions
- Reference something specific about them
- Avoid: "I hope this message finds you well"

### Principle 3: Maximum 1 Question Per DM
- One question per message
- Don't interrogate
- Let the conversation develop naturally

### Principle 4: No "Just Checking In"
- If following up, add value first
- Reference something they posted/shared
- Give something before asking for time

## The 6 Script Types

### SCRIPT 1: Post Engager DM
**Trigger:** Person comments on your post
**Goal:** Continue the conversation, build relationship
**Length:** 2-3 lines
**Template:**
"[Specific thing they said about your post] — totally agree (or [your take]).
[One follow-up question or observation]?"

### SCRIPT 2: Lead Magnet Delivery DM
**Trigger:** Person commented the trigger word for lead magnet
**Goal:** Deliver lead magnet + open conversation
**Length:** 3-4 lines
**Template:**
"Hey [name]! Here's the [lead magnet name]: [link]
[One sentence on what they'll get]
[One question to open conversation]: [question about their situation]?"

### SCRIPT 3: Profile Viewer DM
**Trigger:** Someone viewed your LinkedIn profile
**Goal:** Start dialogue without pitch
**Length:** 2 lines
**Template:**
"Hey [name] — [specific thing about their work that caught your attention].
[One question that shows genuine interest]?"

### SCRIPT 4: Connection Request Follow-Up
**Trigger:** 24 hours after connection accepted
**Goal:** Get a reply, not a sale
**Length:** 3 lines
**Template:**
"Hey [name]! Thanks for connecting.
[One specific thing about their profile/work that resonated].
[One question or conversation starter]?"

### SCRIPT 5: Warm Lead Nurture DM
**Trigger:** Person has engaged multiple times (comments, views, replies)
**Goal:** Transition to call or paid offer
**Length:** 4-5 lines
**Template:**
"Hey [name] — I've noticed you [specific engagement pattern].
[Why you're reaching out specifically].
[Soft offer introduction]: I actually [context about your offer].
[Call to action or question]?"

### SCRIPT 6: Re-engagement DM
**Trigger:** Warm lead went cold (30+ days no response)
**Goal:** Re-open conversation
**Length:** 2 lines
**Template:**
"Hey [name] — [reference to past engagement or shared context].
[New value or perspective]?"

## Conversation Escalation Path

### Stage 1: DM Conversation (D1-D7)
- Keep it light, build rapport
- Ask about their situation
- Share relevant insights

### Stage 2: Value Demo (D7-D14)
- Share relevant content
- Ask if they'd find a specific resource useful
- Gauge interest level

### Stage 3: Soft Offer (D14-D21)
- Mention your offer naturally in conversation
- Ask if they'd be open to hearing about it
- If yes: send details

### Stage 4: Clear Offer (D21+)
- Direct message with offer details
- CTA to book call or learn more
- If no response: wait 30 days, re-engage

## Response Handling

### If They Reply:
- Always respond within 24 hours
- Keep conversation going
- Don't immediately pitch

### If They Don't Reply:
- Wait 5-7 days
- Send one follow-up: different angle
- Then wait 30 days before re-engagement DM

### If They're Not a Fit:
- Thank them for their time
- Ask for referral to someone who might benefit
- Keep connection warm for future

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Each script has a clear singular goal
- [ ] No script opens with "I"
- [ ] No script has more than 1 question
- [ ] Scripts pass "would this creep me out" test
- [ ] Scripts feel handwritten, not templated
- [ ] Conversation escalation path is clear
- [ ] Re-engagement DM is included
