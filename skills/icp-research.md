# ICP Research Skill

ICP frameworks, VOC extraction, and research methodology for the icp-researcher agent.

## ICP Frameworks

### Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD)
What job is the ICP hiring the client's solution to do?
- Functional: specific task they need done
- Emotional: how they want to feel
- Social: how they want to be perceived

### Pain/Gain/Fear Matrix
- Pain: Top 3 frustrations with current solution
- Gain: What would make their life easier
- Fear: What keeps them up at night about their problem

### The 5 Stages of Awareness
1. Unaware — doesn't know they have the problem
2. Problem-aware — knows they have it, doesn't know solution
3. Solution-aware — knows solutions exist, doesn't know yours
4. Product-aware — knows your product, doesn't know if it's right
5. Most-aware — ready to buy, needs only conversion push

## VOC Extraction

### What to Look For
- Exact phrases prospects use (in their words, not industry jargon)
- Specific numbers they mention (time, money, results)
- Emotional language they use around the problem
- Their mental model of how things work

### VOC Sources
- Client's descriptions of what prospects say
- Reviews of competitor products
- Reddit, LinkedIn, forums in their industry
- Sales call recordings if available

### VOC Output Format
Quote the exact phrase, then note:
- Context: when they'd say this
- Emotion: what's driving the statement
- Implication: what this means for positioning

## Psychographics Section

For each psychographic, provide:
- Specific belief or value
- Evidence (why you believe this)
- How this shows up in copy

## Demographics Section

Required fields:
- Job title(s): exact titles they hold
- Seniority: C-suite, VP, Director, Manager, IC
- Company size: employees and/or revenue
- Industry: specific, not broad category
- Geography: specific countries/regions they operate in
- Decision-making role: buyer, influencer, veto power

## Watering Holes

Name specific:
- 3-5 LinkedIn groups they actively participate in
- 2-3 newsletters they subscribe to
- 1-2 podcasts they listen to
- Hashtags they follow (not just generic #business)
- Influencers they respect and follow

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Demographics are specific (named titles, not "decision makers")
- [ ] Psychographics have emotional depth, not just features
- [ ] VOC phrases are verbatim-style, not corporate
- [ ] Watering holes are named specifically
- [ ] Buying triggers are event-based, not generic
- [ ] At least 5 VOC quotes for copywriters to use
